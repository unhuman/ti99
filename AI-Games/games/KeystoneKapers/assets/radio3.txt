Keystone Kapers -- the cathedral radio, 16 x 16 (four 8x8 characters).

SLATS IN TWO BANKS -- the same, broken by a cross-bar

'#' is BLACK ink, '.' is the shop floor showing through (STORE_BG, dark green).
Rows must be exactly 16 characters and there must be exactly 16 of them.

ROWS 0-7 ARE THE CROWN and are NOT yours to change here -- they are RADTL0 and
RADTR0, and rows 0-2 carry the animated sound marks, which are WHITE and have a
second frame (RADTL1/RADTR1) that has to stay in step with them. Edit them in
assets/genart.py if you want to.

ROWS 8-15 ARE THE BODY -- RADBL and RADBR, left half and right half. This is the
part to redraw.

THREE THINGS WORTH KNOWING BEFORE YOU DRAW:

  * TWO COLOURS PER SCAN LINE. Every row here is black plus the floor green, and
    that is the whole budget. A third colour on any row is not available.

  * THE BODY IS FULL WIDTH, so columns 0 and 15 of rows 8-14 are inside the
    cabinet, not outside it. Row 15 is the exception: the gap between the feet is
    genuinely the floor behind the radio.

  * A HOLE TWO PIXELS TALL HAS NO DIRECTION. A field of 2x2 holes reads as a
    checkerboard rather than as a grille, whatever you do with the spacing --
    that draft was rejected. One pixel wide and several tall is what makes the
    eye read slats.

Hand it back and it goes into assets/genart.py as RADBL / RADBR.
