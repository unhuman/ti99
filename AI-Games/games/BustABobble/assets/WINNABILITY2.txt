BUST-A-BOBBLE -- winnability proof, the 50 expert levels, at HARD.

  python3 solvelevels.py --set expert --difficulty hard --depth 160 --overhead 30 --level N

--overhead 30 charges HALF A SECOND OF HUMAN THINKING against every shot: winnable
has to mean winnable by a person, not by a frame-perfect machine.

WHY ONLY HARD IS RECORDED. Harder settings are strictly more pressure -- same board,
same 40 s clock, same shot sequence, only a bigger charge per shot -- so a level
proven at hard is winnable at medium and easy too. The one way that inheritance
could fail is a line that leans on the ceiling-overwrite quirk, which is easier to
trigger with a LOW ceiling and might not reproduce with a high one; the solver
reports those as [N ceiling overwrites] and NONE of the 50 lines below uses it.
(Checked anyway: the arcade set also proves 30/30 at easy and at medium.)
